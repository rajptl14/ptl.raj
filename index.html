Background

Who is Raj?

IMSA grad · Incoming UW–Madison Bio '30 · Independent researcher

I'm an incoming freshman at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, having graduated from the Illinois Mathematics and Science Academy. Over the past few years I've conducted research with Northwestern University's Klein Lab and the University of Illinois Chicago's Minshall Lab — and co-founded AURA, a fundraiser dedicated to early breast cancer detection and awareness.

I'm drawn to problems at the intersection of computation and biology: early disease detection, pattern discovery in complex biological data, and building tools that make scientific insight more accessible. Outside research, I think a lot about science communication and why it matters as much as the science itself.
